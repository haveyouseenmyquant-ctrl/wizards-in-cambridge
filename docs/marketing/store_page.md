## Long description

Dusk on Market Hill. Great St Mary's strikes the hour, and then it strikes once more. Nobody else seems to hear it.

*Wizards in Cambridge* is an action RPG you play in your web browser, set in the real streets of Cambridge city
centre. The city is built from open data: OpenStreetMap for the streets and buildings and Environment Agency LiDAR for
their heights. Walk from Market Hill to King's Parade and Senate House Hill on foot, at a run or on a broom.

**Choose a class.** Four wizards play in four different ways:

- **Pyromancer**: fire and burst damage. Burns linger and explosions stack.
- **Stormcaller**: lightning and wind. Fast and mobile, with chains of lightning; Wind Dash goes further on a broom.
- **Stone Warden**: earth and stone. Shields, stuns and roots; outlasts anything.
- **Hex Weaver**: curses, bound wisps and life drain. Weakens enemies and feeds on them.

Each class has six spells and a three-tier talent tree that changes how those spells behave.

**Fight what lives in the city.** Rooftop gargoyles, the Will-o'-the-Cam, library ink imps, chimney-soot sprites,
clockwork porters and Cam wraiths: twelve kinds of enemy drawn from the city's stone, river and bells, with three
bosses at its landmarks.

**Follow the bells.** Three main quests so far: *The Thirteenth Chime*, *The Lion at the Gate* and *Rung Down*.

**Find better gear.** Loot comes in rarity tiers from drops, chests and bosses, with 58 different equipment effects.
Your level, experience and talents are saved in your browser.

**Or just walk.** Exploration mode turns off the enemies and the HUD so you can wander the city.
